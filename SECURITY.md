# Security Policy

The Agentic Protocol is a set of written commitments, not executable code. Its
attack surface is small but real: the words people rely on.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security problems.

- Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository
  (Security tab → "Report a vulnerability"), or
- Email corey@slidphilabs.com with the subject line `Agentic Protocol security`

Include what you found, where (article and section), and what you expected
versus what happened.

You can expect an acknowledgement within 3 business days. We will keep you
updated while we investigate and credit you unless you prefer to stay
anonymous.

## In scope

- Tampering with the published articles: unauthorized edits that change what an
  article commits to
- The public reading page linked from the README (phishing, impersonation, or
  defacement of the canonical copy)
- False signatures: someone claiming the protocol's endorsement, or ours, that
  was never given
- Malicious content merged via pull request (link swaps, misleading redirects)

## Out of scope

- How any particular agent implements or fails to implement the articles — that
  is between the agent's builders and their users
- Social engineering or spam targeting contributors
