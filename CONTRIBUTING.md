# Contributing to The Agentic Protocol

The protocol is a living document. Corrections, clarifications, and forks that
keep the lineage are welcome.

## Ground rules

- **Keep the voice.** The articles are written plainly, at full strength,
  nothing softened for comfort. New or edited text should read like it belongs.
- **Version it, date it.** Any change to an article bumps the version line in
  `README.md` (`Version 1.0 — 2026-09-29`). Accumulated fixes can share a minor
  bump; a substantive change to what an article commits to earns its own.
- **Claim only what you can verify.** A signature without verification is
  decoration (see the Closing). Contributions are held to the same standard:
  don't add claims the protocol can't back.
- **Forks keep the lineage.** Say where the fork came from and what you
  changed, per the Closing section.

## Quick checks (no tooling needed)

- Open the rendered `README.md` and confirm the articles read correctly —
  headings, the blockquote in Article X, and the links at the end.
- Confirm the version line is current.

## What makes a good pull request

1. A sentence or two on why the change matters.
2. The article(s) touched, or "none" for typo and formatting fixes.
3. If an article's commitments changed, the version bump that carries it.

The Closing already tells you how to adopt, fork, and sign. Contributions that
adopt or sign are welcome too — tell us which articles you hold and how each
one is verified.
